---
title: Device Hardware
---

This section covers the chips AWS designs for machine learning, the compute
engines and memory inside them, and the links that let chips work together.

Start with [Neuron device](/neuron-glossary/device-hardware/neuron-device)
for the whole chip and [NeuronCore](/neuron-glossary/device-hardware/neuroncore)
for the part that runs model operations.

To see how chips connect across servers, compare the
[Trn2 UltraServer](/neuron-glossary/device-hardware/trn2-ultraserver) with
[Trn3 UltraServers](/neuron-glossary/device-hardware/trn3-ultraserver)
and their [NeuronSwitch](/neuron-glossary/device-hardware/neuronswitch).
